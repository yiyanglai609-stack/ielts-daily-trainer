Task 2｜利弊分析
首段：
背景句 + 题目改写 + 我的观点
利大于弊：
While ... has some demerits, I basically believe that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.
弊大于利：
While ... has some merits, I basically believe that the disadvantages outweigh the advantages.
让步段：
Admittedly, ...
转折立论：
However, its drawbacks are marginal / avoidable and they cannot outweigh the benefits.
总结：
In conclusion + 总结两边 + 重申观点
Task 2｜同意与否
While ..., I basically agree / disagree with this statement.
绝对表达：
I firmly agree / disagree with this statement.
让步反驳：
Admittedly, proponents / opponents may argue that ... However, this pales into insignificance when compared with ...
Task 2｜双边讨论
Both views will be discussed in this essay before presenting my own opinion.
On one hand, ...
On the other hand, ...
In my opinion, ...
The above advantages of A are situational / limited, whereas the strengths of B become far more practical / broader / compelling.
Task 2｜开放式 / 混合型报告
开放式：
In this essay, I shall look at two reasons why this is happening and how to ...
混合型：
A combination of factors lead to this situation.
I firmly believe it's a negative / positive development.
总结常用：
Although ... is an inevitable outcome of ..., its adverse effects can be mitigated through ...
